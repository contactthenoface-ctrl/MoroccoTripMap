About this Riad

Nestled deep within the vibrant alleys of the Marrakech Medina, Riad Kasbah offers a peaceful sanctuary away from the bustling souks. Originally built in the 18th century and meticulously restored by local artisans, this authentic Moroccan home blends traditional Moorish architecture with modern luxury.

At the heart of the riad lies a stunning central courtyard, centered around a refreshing plunge pool lined with hand-cut emerald zellige tiles. The scent of orange blossoms and fresh mint tea greets you upon arrival, setting the tone for an unforgettable stay.

Each of our 6 suites is uniquely decorated, featuring intricately carved cedar wood ceilings, tadelakt walls, and locally sourced textiles. Start your day with a traditional Moroccan breakfast served on the panoramic rooftop terrace, which offers breathtaking views of the Koutoubia Mosque and the snow-capped Atlas Mountains.
